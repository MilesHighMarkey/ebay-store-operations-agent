Privacy Policy

PowerPlayGoods Store Operations · Last updated September 29, 2026

This private application is used by PowerPlayGoods to manage its own eBay store operations.

Information used

The application may process eBay account, order, inventory, listing, and product information needed to monitor store performance, calculate profit, and prepare listing drafts.

How information is used

Information is used only for store management, product research, reporting, and approval-gated listing preparation. The application does not sell personal information or provide access to other merchants.

Storage and security

Access credentials are stored as protected application secrets. Store data is limited to what is needed for the application’s functions and is deleted or anonymized when no longer needed.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the PowerPlayGoods store owner through the contact information associated with the eBay account.
